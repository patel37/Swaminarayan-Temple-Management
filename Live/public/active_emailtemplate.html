How to use?

The Cab Drivers app is a subscription-based service application. You can ONLY accept orders IF you are on a subscription plan. If you are not on a subscription plan, you can receive orders BUT you will not be able to accept them. Please ensure that you have enough balance in your e-wallet to purchase any of the subscription plans. Please note that when you are on a subscription plan, regardless if you are ONLINE or OFFILINE, your subscription time will continue to count down
